`inventory.xlsx` has a sheet **Stock** (SKU, Item, Status, Qty, Unit Cost, Value) with a **Total** row at the bottom, and a sheet **Summary** that reports the number of active items, total units and total stock value.

Remove every item whose Status is **Discontinued** from the Stock sheet. Delete the rows entirely; do not leave blank rows.

The Value column, the Total row and the Summary sheet must stay correct for the remaining items, and keep updating (as formulas) when quantities or costs change.
