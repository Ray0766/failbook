`pricing.xlsx` lists our products with their unit **Cost** and selling **Price** (sheet **Products**).

Add a new column **Margin %** in column E, right after Price, with the gross margin of every product:

    Margin % = (Price − Cost) / Price

Requirements:

- Fill it in for every product row.
- Format the column as a percentage.
- It must stay correct when someone later changes a Cost or a Price, so calculate it with formulas.
